Dr. Ning CHEN

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Research Fellow

Department of Computer Science

The University of Exeter, UK

My research focuses on the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and AI for networking, with particular interests in multi-agent cooperative communication, network topology optimization and cusp catastrophe theory.
