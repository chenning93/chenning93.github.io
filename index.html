Difan Zou

Assistant Professor

School of Computing and Data Science

The University of Hong Kong

I am interested in machine learning, stochastic optimization, and graph learning, with a special focus on the theoretical and empirical understanding of deep learning, especially foundation models. I also develop AI/ML methods for practical problems in signal processing, intelligent transportation, and mathematical challenges.
